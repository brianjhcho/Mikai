load-bearing invariant
The graph is the immutable substrate and is read-only to the dream. The wiki is a disposable projection, rebuilt every night — anything it forgets is re-derivable. And the dream holds tensions, never resolves them: unresolved thinking is the highest-value signal, so it is surfaced, not tidied away.
